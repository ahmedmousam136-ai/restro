Our Story

Born from Two Worlds,
Cooked in One Kitchen

Rasōi & Roots was born from the idea that the world's finest cuisines — French, Japanese, American, Indian — share a single truth: great food is made with intention. Our chefs blend tandoor technique with classical French method, steak mastery with the warmth of Indian spice, creating plates that feel both utterly new and like home.
